About KGV

The company behind the ventures.

King Global Ventures (KGV) is a holding company. Under its auspices sit a family of organic brands, a spraying and home care service, a foundation, and an engineering firm, each with its own identity and all on one shared mission.

Our Mission

Why we exist

To drive sustainable growth and economic development by delivering innovative solutions in agriculture and agribusiness, environmental health, engineering, and logistics that empower communities and create long term value.

Supporting commitments

To harness the power of natural materials to promote world food production, environmental stewardship, operational efficiency, sustainability, and a healthier workplace, while being cost effective and socially responsible in creating business opportunities.

To deliver engineering and social engineering consulting, together with equipment sales, rentals and referrals, that support agriculture, mining, ports, construction, roads, highways and bridges.

Our Vision

Where we're going

To build and scale market leading enterprises that power sustainable industrial growth and regional development across West Africa.

Core Values

What we stand on

Our foundation

Integrity

Can we be trusted to do the right thing when no one is watching, when it is costly, or when no rule forces us to? Integrity keeps us aligned with the principles and standards KGV has set, and keeps our communication and reporting honest and consistent under pressure.

Our engine

People

How do we treat the individuals who work for us? We put the safety of our workforce, workplace dignity and employee retention first.

Our systems

Process

Are our results repeatable, scalable and built on reliable systems, or do they depend on luck and individual heroics? Dependable systems keep our operations consistent through staff turnover and changes in leadership.

The outcome

Community Impact

How does our business improve the places where we operate? We focus on community development and environmental responsibility, empowering farmers through farmer centric programs and creating results that can be seen and measured.
