About KGV

The company behind the ventures.

King Global Ventures (KGV) is the parent company harnessing natural materials into products that serve agriculture, sanitation, and land care — through independently branded ventures built on one shared mission.

Our Mission

Why we exist

To harness the power of natural materials to promote world food production, environmental stewardship, operational efficiency, sustainability, and a healthier workplace — while being cost-effective and socially responsible in creating business opportunities.
